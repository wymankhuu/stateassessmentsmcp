## Standards for Mathematical Practice (SMP)

All eight practices apply across all grades and should be integrated into assessment items:

| SMP | Standard | Assessment Integration |
|-----|----------|----------------------|
| **SMP 1** | Make sense of problems and persevere in solving them | Multi-step problems requiring sustained reasoning |
| **SMP 2** | Reason abstractly and quantitatively | Contextualizing/decontextualizing quantities; moving between representations |
| **SMP 3** | Construct viable arguments and critique the reasoning of others | Type 2/SCR items asking students to justify or evaluate a claim |
| **SMP 4** | Model with mathematics | Type 3/PT items with real-world applications |
| **SMP 5** | Use appropriate tools strategically | Calculator policy compliance; choosing methods |
| **SMP 6** | Attend to precision | Correct labels, notation, and degree of precision |
| **SMP 7** | Look for and make use of structure | Pattern recognition; using known structures to solve new problems |
| **SMP 8** | Look for and express regularity in repeated reasoning | Generalizing patterns; making conjectures |

**Assessment note:** SMP 3 (argumentation) is most prominent in Claim 3 / Subclaim C items. SMP 4 (modeling) is central to Claim 4 / Subclaim D items.

## Major vs. Supporting/Additional Content

### Major Content Clusters Receive the Most Assessment Weight

| Grade | Major Content Emphasis (by PARCC/SBAC blueprint) |
|-------|--------------------------------------------------|
| 3 | Multiplication/division, fractions, area |
| 4 | Multiplication/division of multi-digit numbers, fraction operations |
| 5 | Fraction operations, decimal operations, volume |
| 6 | Ratios, rational numbers, expressions and equations |
| 7 | Proportional relationships, rational number operations, linear expressions |
| 8 | Linear functions and equations, geometry transformations, Pythagorean theorem |

**Blueprint rule:** Major content clusters receive approximately 65–70% of assessment points. Supporting and additional content receive 15–25%. Mathematical practices are assessed throughout.

## Calculator and Reference Sheet Policy

| Grade | Calculator | Reference Sheet |
|-------|-----------|----------------|
| 3–5 | No calculator (none of the test) | No reference sheet |
| 6–7 | Four-function calculator (with √ and %) on designated sections | No reference sheet (Gr 6); formulas for Gr 7 |
| 8 | Scientific calculator on designated sections | Formula reference sheet provided |
| 11 | Scientific or graphing calculator | Formula reference sheet provided |

**Assessment design note:** Items that allow calculators should be designed to require conceptual understanding, not just computation. Non-calculator items assess procedural fluency and number sense.

## Assessment Alignment by Claim/Subclaim

| Claim | Content Focus | Primary Standards |
|-------|--------------|-------------------|
| Claim 1 — Concepts & Procedures | Major content at grade level | Domain-specific grade standards |
| Claim 2 — Problem Solving | Application of concepts to solve problems | SMP 1, 4, 5, 6 + grade standards |
| Claim 3 — Communicating Reasoning | Justifying, explaining, critiquing | SMP 3, 6 + grade standards |
| Claim 4 — Modeling & Data Analysis | Real-world modeling and data interpretation | SMP 4 + grade standards + statistics |
