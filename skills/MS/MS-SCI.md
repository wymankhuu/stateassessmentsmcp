## 1. OVERVIEW

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Assessment Name** | MAAP — Mississippi Academic Assessment Program, Science |
| **Administering Agency** | Mississippi Department of Education (MDE) |
| **Vendor** | Questar (ETS successor) |
| **Grades Tested** | **Grade 5 and Grade 8 ONLY** |
| **Testing Window** | April–May (annual summative) |
| **Delivery** | Computer-based testing (CBT); paper accommodations available |
| **Standards** | Mississippi College- and Career-Readiness Standards for Science (2018) |
| **Performance Levels** | 4 levels: Level 1 (Minimal), Level 2 (Basic), Level 3 (Pass/Proficient), Level 4 (Advanced) |
| **Reporting** | Individual Student Reports; School, District, State aggregates |

> **CRITICAL NOTE:** MAAP Science uses the **Mississippi College- and Career-Readiness Standards for Science (2018)**, which incorporate elements of both the Framework for K–12 Science Education and Mississippi-specific content. These standards emphasize three-dimensional learning — Disciplinary Core Ideas (DCIs), Science and Engineering Practices (SEPs), and Crosscutting Concepts (CCCs). Do NOT use generic NGSS PE codes; use Mississippi Science Standards codes.

> **GRADE NOTE:** MAAP Science is administered at **Grades 5 and 8 ONLY**. Mississippi does not currently administer a state science assessment at high school through MAAP (Biology I is tested separately as an end-of-course subject area test). Do not generate MAAP Science items for Grades 3, 4, 6, 7, or high school.

### Performance Levels

| Level | Label | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Minimal** | Student demonstrates minimal understanding of grade-level science standards |
| 2 | **Basic** | Student demonstrates partial understanding; inconsistent application of science concepts and practices |
| 3 | **Pass (Proficient)** | Student demonstrates sufficient understanding of science standards; applies scientific knowledge at grade level |
| 4 | **Advanced** | Student demonstrates thorough, in-depth understanding; exceeds grade-level expectations in scientific reasoning |

## 2. STANDARDS FRAMEWORK

### Mississippi College- and Career-Readiness Standards for Science

Mississippi adopted its own science standards in 2018. The standards are organized around three dimensions: **Disciplinary Core Ideas (DCIs)**, **Science and Engineering Practices (SEPs)**, and **Crosscutting Concepts (CCCs)**.

Standard code format: **[Grade].[Domain].[Standard Number]** or **[Grade Band]-[Domain]-[Standard Number]**

### Three-Dimensional Assessment

MAAP Science items assess students across three dimensions simultaneously:

| Dimension | Description | How Assessed |
|---|---|---|
| Disciplinary Core Ideas (DCIs) | Content knowledge in PS, LS, ESS | The "what" — science content |
| Science and Engineering Practices (SEPs) | Skills such as modeling, analyzing data, constructing explanations | The "how" — what students do with content |
| Crosscutting Concepts (CCCs) | Patterns, cause and effect, systems, energy, stability and change | The "lens" — framework for thinking |

| DOK 1 | Recall facts, definitions, identify | 15–20% |

## 5. ITEM TYPES

### Multiple Choice (MC)

```
+----------------------------------------------------------+
|  MULTIPLE CHOICE — 1 point                               |
|                                                          |
|  4 options (A, B, C, D)                                  |
|  Single correct answer                                   |
|  Machine scored                                          |
|  Scenario-based with data, diagrams, and Mississippi     |
|  phenomena                                               |
|  Both sessions                                           |
+----------------------------------------------------------+
```

**MC Example (Grade 5, DOK 2, 5-ESS2-1 — Earth Systems):**

The Mississippi River carries sediment downstream and deposits it where the river meets the Gulf of Mexico. Over time, this process built the Mississippi Delta. Which Earth systems are PRIMARILY interacting to create the delta?

- A. Atmosphere and biosphere
- B. Hydrosphere and geosphere
- C. Atmosphere and geosphere
- D. Biosphere and hydrosphere

*Correct Answer: B*
*Alignment: 5-ESS2-1 | DOK 2*

---

**MC Example (Grade 8, DOK 2, MS-ESS2-2 — Geoscience Processes):**

Scientists studying the Mississippi River floodplain near Vicksburg found layers of different sediment types in the soil. The bottom layer contained coarse sand, the middle layer contained fine silt, and the top layer contained clay. Which explanation BEST accounts for this arrangement?

- A. Each layer was deposited during a different ice age
- B. Faster-flowing water deposited larger particles first, and slower water deposited finer particles on top over time
- C. Earthquakes shook the particles into layers based on their weight
- D. Plants growing on the surface pushed the smaller particles to the top

*Correct Answer: B*
*Alignment: MS-ESS2-2 | DOK 2*

```

**MS Example (Grade 8, DOK 2, MS-LS2-4 — Ecosystem Changes):**

The Gulf Coast of Mississippi experienced a major oil spill that coated beaches and marshes with petroleum. Which TWO effects would MOST LIKELY result from this event?

- A. The overall amount of energy in the ecosystem would increase
- B. Bird populations that feed on fish in the marsh would decline
- C. Plant growth in the marsh would immediately increase due to oil nutrients
- D. Organisms that depend on clean water for oxygen exchange would be harmed
- E. The salt content of the Gulf water would permanently decrease

*Correct Answers: B and D*

```
Marsh grass --> Insects --> Shorebirds
                   |
Algae --> Shrimp --> Redfish --> Pelicans

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 2 | Accurately and completely addresses the prompt; references data or phenomenon from stimulus; clear scientific reasoning with appropriate vocabulary |

### Suggested Anchoring Phenomena by Grade

**Grade 5 Phenomena:**
- "The Mississippi River deposits millions of tons of sediment each year where it meets the Gulf of Mexico, creating new land. What interactions between Earth's systems cause this process?"
- "After the great Mississippi flood of 2011, farmers noticed that the flooded fields had a new layer of rich, dark soil. Where did this soil come from, and why does it help plants grow?"
- "Scientists studying a Gulf Coast marsh found that marsh grass roots hold soil in place, preventing land from washing away. How do living things interact with nonliving parts of the environment?"

**Grade 8 Phenomena:**
- "The Mississippi Delta has been losing land at a rate of about 25 square miles per year. What natural and human-caused processes explain this land loss, and how could engineering solutions help?"
- "Scientists studying Gulf Coast barrier islands found that the islands shift and change shape after each hurricane season. What geoscience processes are responsible for these changes?"
- "A water quality study found that dissolved oxygen levels in the Gulf of Mexico decrease each summer near the Mississippi River's mouth, creating a 'dead zone.' What biological and chemical processes create this dead zone?"

## 11. QUALITY CHECKLIST AND COMMON PITFALLS

### Quality Checklist

- [ ] Grade verified as 5 or 8 ONLY (not other grades)
- [ ] Standard code uses Mississippi Science Standards format
- [ ] Three-dimensional alignment verified: DCI + SEP + CCC
- [ ] Mississippi-specific phenomenon used in scenario items
- [ ] Multi-Select has exactly 2 correct answers from 5–6 options
- [ ] All MC distractors reflect identifiable student misconceptions
- [ ] Data tables and graphs are clearly labeled with units
- [ ] Science content is accurate and reviewed by subject-matter expert
- [ ] DOK level matches cognitive demand of item
- [ ] Item avoids vocabulary beyond grade-level expectations
- [ ] CR items require evidence-based scientific explanation
- [ ] Performance levels use Mississippi terminology (Minimal through Advanced)

### Common Pitfalls

| Pitfall | Problem | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Writing items for non-tested grades | MAAP Science tests only Gr 5 and 8 | Restrict items to Grades 5 and 8 |
| Using generic NGSS PE codes | Mississippi has its own science standards | Use Mississippi Science Standards codes |
| One-dimensional items (content only) | Mississippi science is three-dimensional | Include SEP and CCC alignment in every item |
| Generic phenomena (not MS-specific) | MS items should anchor in real Mississippi contexts | Use Mississippi River, Gulf Coast, Delta |
| CR without evidence requirement | Three-dimensional items emphasize evidence | Always require students to cite data or observations |
| MS with 3+ correct answers | MAAP MS items use exactly 2 correct answers | Redesign to have exactly 2 correct options |
| Confusing Level 3 (Pass) with Level 2 (Basic) | Level 3 is proficiency target | Target item difficulty to Level 3 performance |
| Including high school items | MAAP Science does not test high school | Only generate items for Grades 5 and 8 |

## 12. PROMPT PATTERNS

The following patterns reflect the most common MAAP Science item structures at Grades 5 and 8.

### Pattern 1: Observation to Explanation (MC, DOK 2)
> "Scientists observed [Mississippi phenomenon]. Which explanation BEST describes why this occurred?"

### Pattern 2: Data Table Interpretation (MC or MS, DOK 2)
> "The table/graph below shows [Mississippi science data]. Based on the data, which [conclusion/statement/pattern] is BEST supported?"

### Pattern 3: Cause and Effect — Ecosystem (MC, DOK 2)
> "In the [Mississippi Delta / Gulf Coast / river ecosystem], [change in one variable] occurred. What would MOST LIKELY be the effect on [another organism or part of the system]?"

### Pattern 4: Experimental Design and Inquiry (MC, DOK 2–3)
> "A student designed an experiment to test whether [variable] affects [outcome] in a Mississippi [context]. Which change would make the experiment MORE valid?"

### Pattern 5: Model-Based Reasoning (CR, DOK 3)
> "Using the [food web / diagram / model] provided, explain how [change or process] affects [system component]. Use evidence from the model and your knowledge of science to support your explanation."

### Pattern 6: Human Impact and Engineering Solutions (MC or CR, DOK 2–3)
> "Mississippi communities along the [river / coast] face [environmental challenge]. Which solution would MOST EFFECTIVELY reduce the impact while considering trade-offs?"
