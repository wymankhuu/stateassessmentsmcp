## 5. ITEM TYPES

### Multiple Choice (MC)

```
+----------------------------------------------------------+
|  MULTIPLE CHOICE — 1 point                               |
|                                                          |
|  4 options (A, B, C, D)                                  |
|  Single correct answer                                   |
|  Machine scored                                          |
|  DOK 1–2                                                 |
+----------------------------------------------------------+
```

**MC Example (Grade 5, DOK 2, 5-ESS2 — Earth's Systems):**

At Hot Springs National Park, thermal water rises from deep underground at a temperature of about 143 degrees F. What is the BEST explanation for why this water is so much warmer than surface water?

- A. The sun heats the water before it enters the ground
- B. Chemical reactions in the water produce heat at the surface
- C. Water circulates deep underground where Earth's internal heat warms it
- D. Friction from water flowing through pipes makes it warm

*Correct Answer: C*

---

### Multi-Select (MS)

```
+----------------------------------------------------------+
|  MULTI-SELECT — 1 point                                  |
|                                                          |
|  5–6 options                                             |
|  Student selects ALL correct answers (typically 2)       |
|  Machine scored; all-or-nothing                          |
|  DOK 2–3                                                 |
+----------------------------------------------------------+
```

**MS Example (Grade 8, DOK 2, 8-ESS2 — Earth's Surface):**

*Blanchard Springs Caverns in the Ozark Mountains formed over millions of years.*

Select TWO processes that contributed to the formation of these caverns.

- A. Volcanic activity pushing rock upward
- B. Slightly acidic water dissolving limestone rock over time
- C. Glaciers carving through the rock
- D. Underground water flowing through cracks and widening them
- E. Wind erosion wearing away the mountain surface

*Correct Answers: B and D*

---

### Constructed Response (CR)

```
+----------------------------------------------------------+
|  CONSTRUCTED RESPONSE — 0–2 points                       |
|                                                          |
|  Written response explaining scientific phenomena        |
|  Must reference data or scenario information             |
|  Human scored using CR rubric                            |
|  DOK 2–3                                                 |
+----------------------------------------------------------+
```

**CR Example (Grade 8, DOK 3, 8-ESS2 — Earth's Surface):**

*"The Arkansas River watershed drains a large area of the state. Using the data provided about sediment levels and water flow at different points along the river, explain how the movement of water shapes the land in the Arkansas River valley. Include at least one specific pattern from the data in your response."*

## 8. SCORING RUBRICS

### Constructed Response (CR) Rubric — 0–2 Points

| Score | Criteria                                                                                                    |
|-------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| 2     | Accurate, complete explanation of the scientific phenomenon; references specific data or evidence; demonstrates clear understanding |
| 1     | Partially accurate explanation; some reference to data or evidence; demonstrates partial understanding       |
| 0     | Inaccurate, irrelevant, insufficient, or blank                                                              |

### Suggested Anchoring Phenomena by Grade

**Grade 5 Phenomena:**
- "At Hot Springs National Park, water emerges from the ground at 143 degrees F — what Earth processes cause this heating?"
- "Students studying an Ozark forest noticed that certain plants only grow in shaded areas near streams — what factors determine where plants grow?"
- "After heavy rains, the Buffalo River turns muddy and brown — what process causes this change in the water?"

**Grade 8 Phenomena:**
- "Blanchard Springs Caverns in the Ozarks contain stalactites and stalagmites that have grown over millions of years — what geoscience processes formed these structures?"
- "Diamond Crater in southeast Arkansas contains volcanic rock unlike anything else in the region — what geological process created this feature?"
- "The Arkansas River carries different amounts of sediment at different points along its path — what factors affect erosion and deposition patterns?"

## 11. QUALITY CHECKLIST AND COMMON PITFALLS

### Quality Checklist

- [ ] Grade verified as 5 or 8 ONLY (ATLAS Science does not test other grades)
- [ ] Standard code uses Arkansas format ([Grade]-[Domain][Standard])
- [ ] Arkansas-specific phenomenon used in scenario items
- [ ] All MC distractors reflect identifiable student misconceptions
- [ ] Data tables and graphs are clearly labeled with units
- [ ] Science content is accurate and grade-appropriate
- [ ] DOK level matches cognitive demand of item
- [ ] Item avoids vocabulary beyond grade-level expectations
- [ ] Environmental contexts are authentic to Arkansas
- [ ] CR items include evidence directive referencing scenario data
- [ ] MS items specify how many options to select
- [ ] Performance level names match Arkansas labels

### Common Pitfalls

| Pitfall                                   | Problem                                           | Fix                                                      |
|-------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------|
| Items for wrong grades                    | ATLAS Science tests only Grades 5 and 8           | Restrict items to Grades 5 and 8                         |
| Generic phenomena (not AR-specific)       | ATLAS items should anchor in real Arkansas contexts | Use Hot Springs, Ozarks, Arkansas River, Diamond Crater  |
| CR rubric exceeds 0–2 range              | ATLAS Science CR items are scored 0–2              | Use the 0–2 rubric scale                                |
| Vocabulary too advanced for grade level   | ATLAS targets grade-appropriate terminology        | Review AR standards for expected vocabulary               |
| Data items without clear labels/units     | Students need labeled data to interpret correctly  | Always include units and clear column/axis labels        |
| MS item not specifying selection count    | Students must know how many to select              | Always include "Select TWO" or similar directive         |
| Wrong performance level labels            | Arkansas uses specific level names                 | Use In Need of Support / Close / Ready / Exceeding      |

## 12. PROMPT PATTERNS

The following patterns reflect the most common ATLAS Science item structures.

### Pattern 1: Ecosystem Interactions (MC, DOK 2, Life Science)
> "In the [Ozark / Buffalo River / Arkansas Delta] ecosystem, [change in one variable] occurred. What would MOST LIKELY be the effect on [another organism or part of the system]?"

### Pattern 2: Data Table Interpretation (MC or MS, DOK 2)
> "The table below shows [Arkansas science data]. Based on the data, which [conclusion / statement / pattern] is BEST supported?"

### Pattern 3: Earth Process Identification (MC, DOK 1–2, Earth Science)
> "A student observed [erosion / weathering / cave formation / thermal activity] at [Arkansas location]. Which process BEST explains the changes the student observed?"

### Pattern 4: Geological Feature Explanation (CR, DOK 3, Earth Science)
> "Using the data provided about [Arkansas geological feature], explain the geoscience process that formed this feature. Include evidence from the data to support your explanation."

### Pattern 5: Weather and Climate Analysis (MC or MS, DOK 2, Earth Science)
> "The [graph / data table] shows weather data recorded in [Arkansas city] over [time period]. Based on the data, what weather pattern is MOST LIKELY occurring?"

### Pattern 6: Adaptation and Survival (MC, DOK 2, Life Science)
> "[Arkansas organism] has [specific physical or behavioral trait]. How does this [adaptation / feature] BEST help the organism survive in its environment?"

### Pattern 7: Experimental Design and Variables (MC or CR, DOK 2–3)
> "A student designed an experiment to test whether [variable] affects [outcome] in an [Arkansas context]. What is the [independent variable / controlled variable / best improvement]?"
